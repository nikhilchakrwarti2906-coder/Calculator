Daily meal plan

  1. Breakfast

    • Main Dish: Pancakes
      • Maple syrup
      • Fresh Berries

  2. Lunch

    • Main course: build your own sandwich

      2. Add a layer of turkish slices.

      4. Finish with lettuce and tomato.

    • Beverage: Iced tea

  3. Dinner

    • Apetizer: Garlic Bread
    • Main course: Spaghetti Bolognese
      1. First, place the cooked Spaghetti.
      2. next, ladle the Bolognese sauce over the pasta.
      3. Finally, sprinkled with grated permesan cheese
    • Dessert: Teramissu
